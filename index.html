About

I learned the rules of numbers as an auditor and the questions as a modeller. Raised in Sharjah, degree in Bangalore, back in Dubai for the work. Days are audit sampling and VAT for gold traders, where the figures are large and the regulator reads them.

Evenings are the two portfolios: twenty investment-banking models in Excel with live formulas and a checks sheet that has to read MODEL OK, and six quantitative studies in Python with tests and out-of-sample results, reported whether or not they flattered the method. I used to compete in esports. Staying calm while a number moves against you turns out to be transferable.

Technology is how I get the volume done, and AI is the colleague I work it through with. The twenty models are generated by Python scripts that pull the filings, build the workbook and run the checks before I open it. At work I built a tool that files goAML compliance reports in batches: eight hundred to a thousand in about five minutes, against the better part of a day by hand. AI does the first pass with me on research, code and drafting; I do the second pass on anything that carries a number. I'm early on whatever the next tool is, not because it's new, but because it lets one analyst do the work of a team and still have time to think.
