Audit · Valuation

Scroll, and I'll value a company in front of you.

STEP 1 / 5 · REVENUE

Start with what comes in.

Five years of sales, forecast from the filings, not from hope.

STEP 2 / 5 · FREE CASH FLOW

Take out what it costs to keep it coming.

Operating costs, tax, capex and working capital. What's left is the cash an owner could actually take.

STEP 3 / 5 · DISCOUNTING

A dollar later is worth less today.

Each year's cash shrinks by the cost of waiting for it: 9% a year, compounding.

STEP 4 / 5 · ENTERPRISE VALUE

Add it up.

Stack the present values, then the years beyond the forecast. That tower is what the business is worth.

STEP 5 / 5 · THE ONE NUMBER

Find the number the price is set on.

Here it isn't revenue or margin. It's the discount rate: one point moves the answer by about a fifth.
